Hi, I am Yiming (Elvin) DU. I am a PhD candidate at the Chinese University of Hong Kong, pursuing my PhD in the Department of SEEM under the supervision of Professor Kam-Fai Wong. I conduct my research at the MoE Key Laboratory of High Confidence Software Technologies (MoE Lab).

From Jan. 2025 to June 2025, I was a visiting researcher at EdinburghNLP, working with Prof. Jeff Z. Pan. I have also been a research intern at Huawei Noah’s Ark Lab (mentored by Baojun Wang), SenseTime Research (mentored by Fei Tan), and previously did research stints at Imperial College London (with Prof. Lucia Specia) and City University of Hong Kong (Web Mining Lab). Prior to CUHK, I obtained my M.Eng. and B.Eng. in Computer Science from Southeast University.

My research lies at the intersection of memory, dialogue, and LLM agents. My long-term goal is to build personal AI assistants that can remember, reason over time, and self-evolve through interaction.

Research Interests

  • Memory for LLM Agents. Long-term & parametric memory, memory operations, retrieval-augmented and reinforcement-learning–based memory reasoning for multi-session agents.
    Representative works: MEMORY-T1 (ICLR 2026), MemGuide (AAAI 2026), PerLTQA (Best Paper, SIGHAN 2024), Rethinking Memory in AI (Survey).

  • Dialogue Systems & Personal Assistants. Multi-turn dialogue modeling, personalization, multi-modal & multi-session conversation.
    Representative works: ReSURE (EMNLP 2025), Sticker Retrieval (AAAI 2025), Flexibly Utilize Memory (EMNLP 2025).

  • Data Selection for LLM Training. High-quality multi-turn data curation, noise-robust SFT/RL training pipelines for Edge LLMs.
    Representative work: ReSURE (EMNLP 2025).
