Hi, I am **Yiming (Elvin) DU**. I am a PhD candidate at the **Chinese University of Hong Kong**, pursuing my PhD in the Department of SEEM under the supervision of <a href='https://www.se.cuhk.edu.hk/people/academic-staff/prof-wong-kam-fai/'>Professor Kam-Fai Wong</a>. I conduct my research at the <a href='https://www1.se.cuhk.edu.hk/~moelab/'>MoE Key Laboratory of High Confidence Software Technologies (MoE Lab)</a>.

From Jan. 2025 to June 2025, I was a visiting researcher at <a href='https://edinburghnlp.inf.ed.ac.uk/'>EdinburghNLP</a>, working with <a href='https://knowledge-representation.org/j.z.pan/'>Prof. Jeff Z. Pan</a>. I have also been a research intern at **Huawei Noah's Ark Lab** (mentored by Baojun Wang), **SenseTime Research** (mentored by Fei Tan), and previously did research stints at **Imperial College London** (with Prof. Lucia Specia) and **City University of Hong Kong** (Web Mining Lab). Prior to CUHK, I obtained my M.Eng. and B.Eng. in Computer Science from **Southeast University**.

My research lies at the intersection of **memory, dialogue, and LLM agents**. My long-term goal is to build personal AI assistants that can remember, reason over time, and self-evolve through interaction.

#### Research Interests

- **Memory for LLM Agents.** Long-term & parametric memory, memory operations, retrieval-augmented and reinforcement-learning–based memory reasoning for multi-session agents.
  <br/>*Representative works:* [MEMORY-T1 (ICLR 2026)](/publications/), [MemGuide (AAAI 2026)](/publications/), [PerLTQA (Best Paper, SIGHAN 2024)](/publications/), [Rethinking Memory in AI (Survey)](https://github.com/Elvin-Yiming-Du/Survey_Memory_in_AI).

- **Dialogue Systems & Personal Assistants.** Multi-turn dialogue modeling, personalization, multi-modal & multi-session conversation.
  <br/>*Representative works:* [ReSURE (EMNLP 2025)](/publications/), [Sticker Retrieval (AAAI 2025)](/publications/), [Flexibly Utilize Memory (EMNLP 2025)](/publications/).

- **Data Selection for LLM Training.** High-quality multi-turn data curation, noise-robust SFT/RL training pipelines for Edge LLMs.
  <br/>*Representative work:* [ReSURE (EMNLP 2025)](/publications/).
